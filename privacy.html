Privacy policy

Crypto MM Journal · last updated 13 November 2026

Crypto MM Journal is a personal Google Apps Script used only by its owner. This policy explains what Google user data it accesses and what it does with it.

Data accessed

  • The owner's own Google Sheet containing the trading journal (read and write).
  • The ability to send email from the owner's account, used only to send the owner their own report.

How data is used

Data is used only to record trading-bot results, build the dashboard, and send summary reports to the owner. It never leaves the owner's Google account except for the report emailed to the owner.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with, or transferred to any third party. No analytics, advertising or tracking is used.

Retention and deletion

Data stays in the owner's spreadsheet until the owner deletes it. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions can be raised as an issue on this site's GitHub repository.
